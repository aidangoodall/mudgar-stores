# Equipment

- Mudgar - the "wine bottle" shaped wooden object, typically with flat sides
- Gada - the mace, Hanuman style
- Indian Club - the words often used here, typically smaller (1-2kg) and used in pairs\
- Samtola - "equal weight", the barbell of India
- Meel, Persian club, similar to the Mudgar, typically wider towards the end

# Practice

- Karlakattai - the Tamil holistic fitness practice (means "wooden clubs"); a kind of warrior fitness
- Padi Karlai
- Meipaadam
- kshakthiriya pranayama - breathing technique used together


# Benefits

- Mindfulness, especially when paired with breathing
- Mind-body connection, unlike simple up and down movements of dumbbells, every aspect of these movements requires conscious effort, leading to greater fine motor control and muscle awareness
- Stability, even holding the club upright activates stabiliser muscles leading to greater control
- Balance, eccentric and concerntic movements, particularly asymmetrical ones improve balance and core strength

- Grip strength, due to large smooth handles, mean constant challenge for grip strength, rather than specific grip exercises
